## Claim ceiling

A verifier-bound attestation PASS establishes stronger provenance/integrity than local behavior PASS.

It does **not** by itself establish:

- independent behavioral review;
- deployment or provider activation;
- protected-effect authority;
- external effect truth merely from Vera's local fence;
- consciousness or phenomenology.

The concrete HMAC verifier is intentionally bounded: possession of the shared secret permits signing, so HMAC provenance is not operational independence.
